The Terms

Creator Marketplace Agreement

Revenue split. You keep 85% of every sale. The Lazy Creator Co. keeps 15% as the marketplace fee. No other cuts, no tiered pricing based on volume — the split is the same whether you sell one copy or a thousand.

Listing goes live immediately. There's no waiting period or manual review queue before your product appears. Once you submit and it passes the checklist below, it's live. The Lazy Creator Co. reserves the right to remove any listing after the fact — for content that doesn't fit the brand, quality concerns, or terms violations — but that's a post-publish action, not a pre-publish gate.

Content standards. Everything sold here has to actually be faith-forward or clearly serve the audience this brand exists for — creators, educators, families, and people building something with their own hands. No AI-generated spam, no reskinned/reselling of someone else's work, no content that contradicts the "does this point to God, or just to a checkout page?" filter every other product here gets measured against.

Ownership. You keep full ownership of what you make. Submitting a product here grants The Lazy Creator Co. a license to list, market, and sell it on this site — not a transfer of ownership or exclusivity. You're free to sell the same product anywhere else too.

Payouts. Payment mechanics (schedule, method, and minimum payout threshold) are still being finalized and will be confirmed directly with approved creators before your first sale. This page will be updated once that's locked in.

This is a plain- language summary of the working terms, not a substitute for a formal signed agreement. A complete written contract will be provided before any payout is issued.
