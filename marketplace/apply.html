The Terms

Creator Marketplace Agreement

Flat listing fee, no revenue split. You pay $12/month or $99/year to have your product listed here. That's it. No percentage of your sales, no tiered pricing based on volume. Every sale happens on your own store (Gumroad, Etsy, wherever your product already lives) and every dollar of it is yours. The Lazy Creator Company never touches your checkout or your revenue.

Listing goes live on payment. There's no waiting period or manual review queue. Submit this form, then complete payment on the next page, your listing publishes automatically the moment payment is confirmed. The Lazy Creator Company reserves the right to remove any listing after the fact (for content that doesn't fit the brand, quality concerns, or terms violations), but that's a post-publish action, not a pre-publish gate.

Content standards. Everything listed here has to actually be faith-forward or clearly serve the audience this brand exists for, creators, educators, families, and people building something with their own hands. No AI-generated spam, no reskinned/reselling of someone else's work, no content that contradicts the "does this point to God, or just to a checkout page?" filter every other product here gets measured against.

Ownership. You keep full ownership of what you make. Submitting a product here grants The Lazy Creator Company a license to list, describe, and link to it from this site, not a transfer of ownership or exclusivity, and not a right to sell it directly. You're free to list the same product anywhere else too.

Billing. Your listing fee renews automatically through Stripe ($12/month or $99/year, whichever you choose) until cancelled. To cancel, email thelazycreatorco@gmail.com and your listing will be removed at the end of the current billing period.

This is a plain- language summary of the working terms, not a substitute for a formal signed agreement.
