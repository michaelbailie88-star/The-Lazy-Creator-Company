Leaks Plumbing

A local trades site concept mockup — built around the single question every homeowner has when something's leaking: can I trust this person to show up and do it right?

View Live Mockup →

The Problem

Trades businesses live or die on trust, and most local plumber/contractor sites do nothing to build it — no licensing info up front, no clear service list, and reviews (if they exist at all) buried three clicks deep instead of front and center where a nervous homeowner needs them.

The Solution

A trust-first layout: a credibility bar right at the top (licensed, insured, years in business), a clear service grid so visitors immediately know what's covered, and real customer reviews placed high enough to actually get read before someone decides to call.

Tech Stack

HTML / CSS / JS Hand-coded, no page builder Mobile-first Netlify-ready

What This Is Designed To Do

This is a concept mockup, not a live client site, so there are no real call-volume numbers to report. What it demonstrates: leading with trust signals instead of burying them, a service grid that answers "do they do what I need" in one glance, and a layout built for someone searching in a panic on their phone, not browsing casually on a laptop.
