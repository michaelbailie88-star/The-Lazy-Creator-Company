Why Larios

A home kitchen, held to a real standard.

Larios Cake & Co. is a Guelph-based custom cake business built around one idea: every cake should feel personal, not picked off a shelf. Whether it's a loaded drip cake stacked with chocolate bars or a fully sculpted fondant design, every order starts with a conversation about your event, your flavours, and what you're picturing.

No walk-in counter, no pre-made display case — just cakes built to order, one celebration at a time.
