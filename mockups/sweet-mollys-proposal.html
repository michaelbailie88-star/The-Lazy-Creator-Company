Three Shops in One

Not just a bakery. Not just a grocer.

Sweet Molly's is the rare kind of place that does all three well — the pantry staples you can't find anywhere else, the bakery case that smells like it should, and a spot to sit down with a proper pot of tea.

01 — The Shop

British & Newfoundland Imports

Biscuits, jams, teas, cheeses, chocolate bars, and pantry goods you grew up on — plus a corner stocked for Newfoundland expats who miss home just as much.

02 — The Bakery

Baked Fresh, Every Day

Meat pies, sausage rolls, scones, Victoria sponge, and shortbread — made the way it's supposed to be made, not shipped in from somewhere else.

03 — The Tea Room

A Proper Sit-Down Cuppa

Pull up a chair, order a pot of tea and a scone with clotted cream, and take your time. No rushing you out the door here.

A Local Landmark

Meet Molly — the moose out front everyone in Acton knows

Sweet Molly's isn't just named after its owner. Out front stands a big white moose statue that's become a genuine piece of local history — she's been part of the neighbourhood, been through a rough patch, and the town rallied to bring her back.

It's the kind of small-town detail that makes people stop, take a photo, and remember exactly where the shop is. Come say hello to Molly, then come inside for the tea.
