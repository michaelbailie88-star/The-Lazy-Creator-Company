Our Story

Pizza with a history. We deliver happiness.

We aren't like other pizza shops. We're a family business focused on value and quality. With great prices, real ingredients, and service that remembers your name, we believe everything you need is right here on Dovercliffe Road.

From regular dough to whole wheat to gluten-free, the possibilities are endless, and every pizza still gets Tito's personal touch.
