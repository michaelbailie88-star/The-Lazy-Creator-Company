Why Larios

A home kitchen, held to a real standard.

Larios Cake & Co. is a Guelph-based custom cake business built around one idea: every cake should feel personal, not picked off a shelf. Every order starts with a conversation about your event, your flavours, and what you're picturing — then it's baked from scratch and finished by hand.

No walk-in counter, no pre-made display case — just cakes built to order, one celebration at a time.

About This Concept

Hi — this is a concept, built specifically for you.

I put this together using photos from your own Instagram (@larioscakeco) to show what a real site could look like — not a template, not a generic bakery layout, just your actual work presented the way it deserves to be seen.

What this gives you that Instagram alone can't:

  • A place people can land on and immediately see your range — cakes, cupcakes, cookies — without scrolling a grid
  • A clear, honest ordering process so new customers know exactly how to reach you and what to expect
  • Something you can link in your bio, share in a story, or send directly to someone asking "do you have a website?"

This is a working concept, not a finished, hosted site yet — happy to talk through pricing, timeline, and what's involved in making this real if it's something you'd want.
