Larios Cake & Co.

A custom cake bakery concept mockup — built for a made-to-order business with no walk-in storefront, not a live client engagement.

The Problem

A lot of small custom-cake bakers run entirely on Instagram — no separate site, no way for someone to see the full range of what's offered, and no clear path for how ordering actually works. That's fine for followers already in the loop, but it's a dead end for anyone finding the business for the first time and not sure whether to DM, call, or just scroll away.

The Solution

A site built around the actual shape of a made-to-order business: no product catalog or checkout, because that's not how custom cakes get sold. Instead, a clear "what we bake" breakdown, an honest three-step ordering process, and a direct path to starting a conversation — the same way the business already operates, just easier to find.

Tech Stack

HTML / CSS / JS Hand-coded, no page builder Mobile-first Netlify-ready

What This Is Designed To Do

This is a concept mockup, not a live client site, so there are no real order numbers to report. What it demonstrates: a layout that fits a made-to-order business model instead of forcing an e-commerce template onto it, an ordering process that sets expectations before anyone reaches out, and a gallery section built to plug in real cake photography the moment it's available.
