Tier 1 • Static Product + Automated Production Pipeline

TLC Library

A journal or devotional line needs a consistent visual identity and fast, repeatable production, without needing a designer commissioned per title.

The Problem

Publishing a multi-title devotional or journal line by hand, one title at a time with a designer, doesn't scale, and it's easy for visual and content standards to drift between titles without an enforced system.

What Was Built

The Renewed adult devotional journal line, alongside a standing automated production pipeline (book_engine.py) that enforces a locked visual and content standard, Cinzel engraved gold titles, warm radial vignette covers, consistent structure, across every title produced.

Tech Stack

Python production pipeline Canva-based design system Enforced visual/content standard

Status

Live, payment-integrated. 3 of 18 planned titles complete.
