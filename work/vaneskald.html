Tier 3 • Autonomous AI Content Engine

Vaneskald

Sustained multi-platform content output is effectively a full-time job most solo operators and small teams can't sustain manually.

The Problem

Consistent daily posting across multiple platforms, each with its own format expectations, is one of the biggest bottlenecks for any content-driven brand. Hiring a full-time content team isn't viable for most solo operators or small teams.

What Was Built

A fully autonomous pipeline that writes the script, generates the voiceover, renders the finished video through Creatomate, and auto-posts it across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube through Buffer, entirely on schedule, with nobody touching a camera or an upload button.

Tech Stack

Creatomate rendering Buffer scheduling/posting Autonomous script + voice generation

Status

Live at vaneskald.com, currently in its audience-building phase.
