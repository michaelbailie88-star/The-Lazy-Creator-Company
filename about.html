About

Kingdom-first, then building

The Lazy Creator Company is built and run by Michael Bailie, a Kingdom-first creator who also builds, in that order.

It started the way most honest things do: with a question I kept asking myself before posting, before building, before shipping anything at all: does this point to God, or just to a checkout page? That question is still the filter for everything that carries The Lazy Creator Company name.

Faith-forward content has always been the real center of this brand, even on the days it got crowded out by the business-building side of things. The Faith Library exists to put that center back in the middle where it belongs: a growing line of yearlong devotional journals for the mornings that need something steady.

“But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.”

Matthew 6:33 (KJV)

Alongside the Faith Library, Activity Vault represents the other half of what "building" looks like here, a genuinely useful, educator-built tool, developed closely with a Senior Childhood Educator with twenty years in early-childhood development. Different audience, different purpose, same underlying belief: build things that actually serve the people using them.

“Kingdom first, then building. In that order, every time.”

Michael Bailie, Founder

That belief doesn't stop at two products. Everything below is either live today or genuinely being built toward, not a portfolio of ideas, an actual accounting of where things stand.
