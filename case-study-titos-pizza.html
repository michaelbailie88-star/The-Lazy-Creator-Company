Tito's Pizza

A restaurant concept mockup, built to show what a local pizza shop's site could look like, not a live client engagement.

View Live Mockup →

The Problem

Most small restaurant sites are built for the owner, not the customer, a photo-heavy homepage and a phone number, when what most visitors actually want in the first three seconds is the menu, the hours, and a way to order.

The Solution

A menu-first layout: hours and location visible without scrolling, a clean menu that doesn't require a PDF download, and a clear path toward ordering or calling, built mobile-first, since most restaurant searches happen on a phone at dinnertime.

Tech Stack

HTML / CSS / JS Hand-coded, no page builder Mobile-first Netlify-ready

What This Is Designed To Do

This is a concept mockup, not a live client site, so there are no real order-volume numbers to report. What it demonstrates: a menu-first layout that answers hours, location, and "what's on the menu" without scrolling, and a mobile-first build since most restaurant searches happen on a phone at dinnertime.
